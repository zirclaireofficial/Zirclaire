1. Definitions
1.1. “Platform” refers to the website, application, and services operated by the Platform Administrator.
1.2. “Service Provider” refers to a registered user offering services through the Platform.
1.3. “Service Requester” refers to a registered user purchasing services through the Platform.
1.4. “Project” refers to any service engagement agreed upon between a Service Provider and a Service Requester through the Platform.
1.5. “Project Fund” refers to the total amount paid by the Service Requester for a Project.
2. Account Registration Requirements
2.1 Service Provider Requirements
Before creating an account on the Platform, every Service Provider must:
a) Maintain an active Touch ’n Go (TNG) account;
b) Provide accurate personal information and verification documents requested by the Platform;
c) Agree to all Platform policies and procedures.
The Platform reserves the right to reject or suspend any account that fails to comply with these requirements.
2.2 Service Requester Requirements
Before creating an account on the Platform, every Service Requester must:
a) Maintain an active Binary Account approved by the Platform;
b) Provide accurate registration information;
c) Accept these Terms and Conditions.
SECTION 3 – PROJECT CREATION AND MANAGEMENT
3.1 Creation of Projects
A Service Requester may create and publish one or more Projects through the Platform. There shall be no maximum limit on the number of Projects that a Service Requester may create, provided that each Project complies with these Terms and all applicable Platform policies.
Each Project shall include, at a minimum:
a. A clear title and description of the services required;
b. The scope of work and expected deliverables;
c. The Project Duration, including the commencement date and completion deadline;
d. The applicable Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), specifications, technical requirements, or acceptance criteria;
e. The total Project Fund payable for the Project; and
f. Any other information required by the Platform.
The Platform reserves the right to reject, suspend, remove, or modify any Project listing that is incomplete, misleading, unlawful, fraudulent, or otherwise inconsistent with these Terms.
3.2 Project Funding Requirement
Every Project shall be fully funded before it becomes available for acceptance by a Service Provider.
The Service Requester shall deposit one hundred percent (100%) of the Project Fund using the approved Binary Account designated by the Platform.
No Project shall commence until the full Project Fund has been successfully received and confirmed by the Platform.
3.3 Escrow of Project Funds
Upon successful receipt of the Project Fund, the Platform shall hold the Project Fund in escrow until:
a. the Project is successfully completed;
b. the Project is cancelled;
c. the Project expires as an Unclosed Project;
d. a refund becomes payable; or
e. a dispute has been fully resolved in accordance with these Terms.
Neither the Service Provider nor the Service Requester shall have access to the escrowed Project Fund except as provided under these Terms.
3.4 Acceptance of Projects
A Service Provider may accept a Project only through the Platform.
Upon acceptance of a Project:
a. the Project shall become an Active Project;
b. the Project Duration shall commence;
c. the Service Provider shall become responsible for completing the Project in accordance with the agreed scope, timeline, SOPs, and these Terms.
3.5 Project Communication
All communications relating to a Project should be conducted through the Platform’s messaging or communication system wherever reasonably possible.
The Platform may rely upon communications conducted through the Platform as evidence during dispute resolution.
Communications conducted outside the Platform shall be at the Users’ own risk and may not be recognised during dispute investigations.
3.6 Modification of Projects
Any modification to the Project scope, deliverables, completion deadline, or Project Fund shall require the mutual agreement of both the Service Provider and the Service Requester through the Platform.
The Platform may refuse any modification that would violate these Terms or compromise the integrity of the Platform.
3.7 Prohibited Projects
Users shall not create, offer, request, or perform Projects that:
a. violate any applicable law or regulation;
b. infringe intellectual property rights;
c. involve fraudulent, deceptive, or misleading conduct;
d. involve money laundering, terrorist financing, or other financial crimes;
e. promote hate, violence, discrimination, or unlawful activities;
f. require the disclosure of confidential information without lawful authority; or
g. otherwise violate the Platform’s policies.
The Platform may immediately remove any prohibited Project without prior notice.
SECTION 4 – SERVICE PROVIDER PROJECT CAPACITY

To maintain service quality and timely completion, a Service Provider may undertake a maximum of three (3) Active Projects simultaneously.

Upon the completion, cancellation, refund, expiration, or closure of an Active Project, the corresponding Project slot shall automatically become available.
The Service Provider may immediately accept another Project, provided that the total number of Active Projects does not exceed three (3).
For example:
a. If a Service Provider has accepted three (3) Active Projects, no additional Projects may be accepted.
b. If two (2) of those Projects are completed or otherwise closed, the Service Provider may accept two (2) additional Projects.
4.3 Restriction on Additional Projects
The Platform shall automatically prevent a Service Provider from accepting a fourth (4th) Active Project while already managing three (3) Active Projects.
Any attempt to circumvent this limitation may result in suspension or termination of the Service Provider’s account.
4.4 Service Requester Project Capacity
There shall be no restriction on the number of Projects that a Service Requester may create or fund, provided that:
a. each Project complies with these Terms;
b. each Project is fully funded before publication;
c. the Service Requester remains in good standing with the Platform; and
d. all Projects are lawful and do not violate the rights of any person.
4.5 Platform Authority
The Platform reserves the right to increase, reduce, suspend, or otherwise modify the maximum number of Active Projects permitted for any Service Provider where reasonably necessary for:
a. risk management;
b. quality assurance;
c. performance monitoring;
d. fraud prevention;
e. account verification status;
f. operational requirements; or
g. compliance with applicable laws or regulations.
The Platform shall endeavour to notify affected Users of any such modification where reasonably practicable.
4.6 Abuse Prevention
Users shall not manipulate, duplicate, divide, or otherwise structure Projects or accounts for the purpose of circumventing the Project capacity limits or any other restrictions imposed by the Platform.
